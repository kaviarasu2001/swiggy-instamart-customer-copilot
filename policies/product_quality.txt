MOCK INSTAMART PROJECT POLICY: PRODUCT QUALITY

This is a simulated policy created for the academic project.

1. Customers should report damaged, defective, or poor-quality products.
2. The support team should collect order details and a description of the issue.
3. Photographic evidence may be requested when appropriate.
4. Eligible complaints should be reviewed for a suitable resolution.
5. Serious quality complaints should be escalated to the Quality Support team.
