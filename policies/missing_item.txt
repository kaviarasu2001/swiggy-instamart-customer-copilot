MOCK INSTAMART PROJECT POLICY: MISSING ITEM

This is a simulated policy created for the academic project.

1. Customers should report missing items from their order.
2. The support team should verify the order details and item list.
3. The case should be assigned to Order Support.
4. Eligible cases may be considered for a refund or other resolution after review.
5. Repeated or disputed missing-item complaints should be escalated.
